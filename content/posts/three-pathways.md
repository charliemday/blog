## Von Neumann Completionists

These will be the people obsessed with "completing the map". Remember when you finished the story mode of a video game and then the only reason to go back to the game would be to unlock every single collectible, uncover every hidden part of the map, find every easter egg?

That's the kind of people who will be Von Neumann Completionists. They want to visit every point in the solar system, the galaxy, and the local cluster. They'll want to tick off every species they find on every planet. They'll want to "complete" every star system. Most people probably won't want to do this simply because it requires a lot of waiting. There's a lot of space between Planet Earth and Alpha Centauri. 

4 light years of space. 

In fact, for the most part, space isn't actually that interesting. 

It's literally space. You couldn't get anything more boring. 

It has small pockets of **extremely** interesting parts.

This pathway will likely be reserved for the rich obsessives.   

*"I think that what you don't understand, Mustafa, is that by the end of the century, I'm going to be exploring the galaxies at the head of a von Neumann probe."* 

## Amish Luddites

So you like the world as it was pre-industrial. Minimalistic tech. No electricity. No cars. No planes. No internet. Just horses, farming, and basic tools. 

This is where you find your meaning. You probably also hold onto the old ideas of religion, family structure, community, and nature.

There'll be nature reserves carved out on planets for these people. Far removed from the data centres and massive solar panels running them. 

## The Data Gods

99.9% of the population will take this option: becoming data gods. 

These are people living in data centres. Instead of having organic brains, they'll just have silicon brains. They'll be able to live out their wildest fantasies and construct realities.

They'll explore never-before-seen stories and ideas which will become the new currency. Instead of dollars and yen we'll have stories and experiences you can trade.  

They'll be able to change what they remember, to trick themselves into being mortal. To find meaning in the infinite world of imagination. 

Spoiler: you're probably already there.
